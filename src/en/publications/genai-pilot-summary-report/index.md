This report summarizes outcomes from a six-month generative AI pilot
conducted with three federal departments, migrated from the AI Centre of
Excellence's pilot reports archive.

## Overview

The pilot tested generative AI assistance for drafting internal
correspondence, summarizing meeting notes, and triaging service requests.

## Findings

- Time savings averaged 22% on drafting tasks.
- Human review remained mandatory for all external-facing content.
- Bilingual output quality required additional prompt tuning for French.

## Recommendations

Departments considering similar pilots should budget time for prompt
engineering in both official languages and establish a review workflow
before scaling beyond a pilot cohort.
