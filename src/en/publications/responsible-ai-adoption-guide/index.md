This guide summarizes the core practices program teams should follow when
evaluating and adopting AI tools, migrated from the AI Centre of Excellence's
internal knowledge base.

## Why this matters

Federal programs are increasingly exploring AI-enabled solutions. This guide
distills lessons learned across pilot projects into a short set of
recommendations.

## Key recommendations

1. Start with a clearly defined business problem, not a technology.
2. Complete an Algorithmic Impact Assessment (AIA) early.
3. Document data sources, model limitations, and human oversight points.
4. Plan for bilingual, accessible user experiences from day one.
